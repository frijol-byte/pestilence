# La Crypte de Saint-Roch (test scene) + module skeleton
**Date:** 2026-09-26 10:14 · **Version:** 0.1.0

## Added
- Module skeleton `pestilence` (Foundry v14, PF2e 8.5.1), installable by manifest URL.
- Scene: **La Crypte de Saint-Roch** — `src/maps/crypte-saint-roch.mjs`, compendium
  `pestilence-scenes`. 36×24 squares, 86 walls (4 doors: 2 normal, 1 locked iron, 1 secret),
  10 lights, darkness 1, token vision on.

## Areas
- **A. Stair hall** — "Saint Roch · priez pour nous"; red plague cross chalked by the nave door.
- **B. Nave of the Dead** — quatrefoil piers, effigy tombs, skull niches, seven-branched
  candle stand; one sarcophagus opened from inside. "Ici gisent ceux que la peste a pris."
- **C. Chapel of Saint Roch** — beheaded statue of the saint and his dog, *memento mori* mosaic.
- **D. The Lime Pit** — mass grave in quicklime, dead-cart, dropped plague-doctor mask.
- **E. The Inverted Sanctum** — secret door → locked iron door; heptagram (seven sins),
  *Dies Irae* written backwards, black altar, inverted cross, emptied reliquary.

## Design notes
- Built before the CLAUDE.md scene rules existed: no named demon/devil, fights, puzzles or
  journal yet. Corridors are 5 ft (claustrophobic).
