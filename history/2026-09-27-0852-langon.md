# Chapter I: Langon (the bridge and the abbey)
**Date:** 2026-09-27 08:52 · **Version:** 0.3.0

Party: 4 PCs, level 2. Smaller scene (not `full_scene`).

## Added
- **Journal:** *III. Langon* (`src/content/journals/ch1-langon.mjs`)
  - *Arrivée à Langon*: crossroads town ruined by the war (taken twice by French and English
    companies) and then by the plague; gallows with a split fleur-de-lis shield; nothing of value.
  - *Le Pont*: the witch drowned by the town (sentence nailed to the Saint Nicholas shrine);
    Perception DC 15 to spot her; Athletics DC 18 to haul her up (dive DC 15 lowers it to 14);
    three attempts before her charm fails.
  - *L'Abbaye Saint-Gervais*: approach "wrongness" (flies, no birds, warm holy water; Religion/
    Perception DC 14); Père Clément's blasphemies; the unholy communion; the unmasking; 20 gp of
    trinkets and donations; the reconciliation ritual.
  - *Onward*: hooks east.
- **Scene:** *L'Abbaye Saint-Gervais (Langon)* (`src/maps/abbaye-saint-gervais.mjs`), 32×21, church
  only, standing but desecrated: west porch and double doors, aisled nave with arcades,
  choir with wine casks and the abbot's chair, fallen inverted rood, black altar in the apse,
  plundered sacristy, bell tower with the cut-down bell. Darkness 0.55; 3 lights.
- **Actors** (`src/content/actors/langon.mjs`):
  - **Bacbuc** ("Père Clément"), L3 cambion, squire of Beelzebub's Order of the Fly. 60 XP (Low).
    *Mechanical weakness:* weakness holy 5, Will +7, starts Sickened 2 and never retches.
    *Lore weakness:* True Sacraments Burn (holy water, blessed wine, a true blessing).
    Priestly Guise (Religion DC 16 to see through), Unholy Communion, Spit Burning Wine.
  - **Guilhelma la Masca**, L1 cunning woman; *Remède de la Masca*: cures La Peste Noire once.
- **Items** (`src/content/items/langon-items.mjs`):
  - **Effect: Communion Impie**: −1 status to Will saves and to saves against unholy effects;
    ended by true confession or the church's reconciliation.
  - **Réconciliation d'une Église**: rank 2 uncommon ritual, 3 hours, champion/divine primary
    caster, Religion DC 18 primary, Religion or Crafting DC 16 secondary, costs Gregorian water
    (holy water + salt + ashes + wine). Based on the real rite of reconciling a profaned church.
- **Library:** `effect()` and `ritual()` builders; NPC `senses`; new church props and floor
  features in the renderer.

## Design notes
- Sin tracker: +1 saving Guilhelma; −1 harming her after rescue; −1 per PC who takes the
  communion; +1 defeating Bacbuc; +1 reconciling the church.
- "Cambion" is a kind of devil (child of an incubus and a mortal woman), not a name (per the user).
- Names: *Bacbuc* is Rabelais' oracle of the Holy Bottle; "Père Clément" mocks Pope Clement VI;
  *masca* is Occitan for witch; Saint-Gervais is Langon's real parish dedication.
- New threads: the **Order of the Fly** (Bacbuc's master, a knight-commander riding east);
  Guilhelma (possible ally, suspect, or victim of the penitents).
